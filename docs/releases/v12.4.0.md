# Not-Noop v12.4.0

Better use of a foldable's second half, and a small fix on Today.

## Book mode: two columns

Half-open a foldable and hold it like a book (hinge running top to bottom):

- **Today stays on one side**, always in view.
- **The other side shows the tab you pick** on the sidebar: Trends, Sleep, Workouts, Coach or More. It
  starts on Sleep (last night).
- **Details open beside Today.** Tapping the Charge, Effort or Rest ring, a metric tile, the journal,
  Sleep, the battery ring or your avatar opens that screen on the other side instead of replacing Today.
- **Swap sides.** A button under the sidebar's icons flips which side Today is on.
- **Back** works as usual on the other side; from one of the main tabs it closes the app, as Back
  from Today does.
- Folding the phone flat, or closing it, keeps whatever you were looking at on the other side as the
  full screen.

Phones, tablets, the half-folded "tabletop" layout and a flat-unfolded foldable are unchanged.

## Fix

- **"Good evening" is no longer cut short** next to the readiness and SOLID pills on Today. The
  greeting was only given half of the free space in that row.

Built and checked with the Android unit test suite. Book mode is new and not yet tested on every
foldable; please use Settings › Support › Report a bug if something looks wrong.
