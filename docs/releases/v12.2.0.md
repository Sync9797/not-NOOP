# Not-Noop v12.2.0

The first release of Not-Noop, a community maintenance fork of NOOP (the offline WHOOP companion) that
focuses on fixing bugs in the Android app. It is built on NOOP 12.1.0 from
[ryanbr/noop](https://github.com/ryanbr/noop) and keeps everything on your own device: no account, no
cloud.

Not-Noop installs **beside** the original NOOP app (its app id is `com.not.noop.oop`), with its own
data. It is still called "NOOP" on the launcher.

## Health Connect

- **Sharing no longer stuck on "Sharing paused — permission was revoked".** The writer asked for VO2 max
  write access, but the app never declared that permission, so Health Connect could never grant it and
  every VO2 max write failed. It is declared now. VO2 max is also optional: if you decline it, the rest
  of sharing carries on instead of pausing.
- **Imported workouts get their distance.** The importer asked for distance read access, which was also
  never declared, so workouts from Samsung Health, Garmin and other apps always showed 0 distance.
- **Synced days keep updating.** A day imported once was treated as belonging to the strap and never
  refreshed, so sleep your watch uploaded after that sync never appeared. It now refreshes, and a night
  whose times changed is replaced instead of shown twice.
- **Sync retries after a failure** instead of waiting a whole interval, and runs every time you return
  to the app, not only on a cold start.
- **No double counting from the original NOOP.** Data the original app (or the demo build) shared into
  Health Connect is skipped on import.

## Tablets, foldables and landscape

- On wide screens a glass sidebar on the right replaces the bottom bar. It floats over your background
  and content never goes under it.
- Screen content stays a readable width instead of stretching edge to edge.
- Half-folded (tabletop), the screen sits above the hinge with the navigation below it.
- The status bar stays one colour across the full width.
- Phones are unchanged.

## Strap vibrations

- Notifications default to a **single** buzz, calls to a **long** one. Per-app choices you already
  made are kept. On a WHOOP 4.0 every buzz is the strap's built-in alarm buzz, so only the number of
  buzzes can change.
- **Tap for time** reads hours, a pause, then quarter hours (3:25 is three buzzes, a pause, one). It
  waits for each buzz to finish so they no longer run together. The digit reading is still available
  under Settings.

## Look and feel

- **Material You:** an optional "Use wallpaper colours" accent on Android 12 and later.
- Every standard screen now shows your chosen sky or background image, with readable titles.

## Other

- Support in Settings opens a bug report on this repository.
- The update check looks at this repository's releases.

Built and checked with the Android unit test suite. Not yet tested on every device; please report what
you find.
