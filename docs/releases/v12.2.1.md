# Not-Noop v12.2.1

A small update so AI Widget Maker's new widgets can show NOOP's own data.

## AI Widget Maker

- **Stress and Coach brief widgets.** NOOP now shares two more things with AI Widget Maker (and only
  with that app, on your phone; nothing leaves the device): today's stress curve, on NOOP's 0–3 scale,
  and the latest Coach brief with when it was written. AI Widget Maker 0.9.13 or later uses them for
  its Stress and Coach brief widgets.
- **Stress is scored for those widgets.** NOOP used to score today's stress only while one of its own
  stress widgets was on the home screen. It now also does so while AI Widget Maker is installed, so its
  Stress widget fills without a NOOP widget placed.
- **A new Coach brief appears straight away** in AI Widget Maker, instead of on the next heart-rate
  update.

Everything else is unchanged from 12.2.0.
