# Not-Noop v12.3.1

Two layout fixes found on a real foldable after 12.3.0.

- **Big buttons when half-folded.** Standing a foldable on its hinge (tabletop) used to leave the
  bottom half almost empty, with the phone's thin bar floating in it. That half now holds large
  rounded buttons for Today, Trends, Sleep, Coach and More. The current screen's button is outlined in
  your accent colour.
- **Headings readable on your background.** With a background photo, or "Sky behind cards" on,
  section headings that sit directly on the background, such as Sleep's "Last night / Sleep
  performance", now use light text with a soft shadow, like the screen titles. They used to be dark
  and nearly vanished on a dark photo. Headings inside cards are unchanged.

Everything else is unchanged from 12.3.0.
