# Not-Noop v12.3.0

New options you can turn on if you want them, and layout polish for foldables and tablets. Nothing
changes how NOOP scores your data.

## Health Connect

- **Optional background sync.** Data Sources › auto-sync has a new **Sync in the background** switch.
  It is **off by default**: NOOP normally reads Health Connect only while it is open, which saves
  battery and means your health data is not read while the app is closed. Opening NOOP still syncs.
  Turn it on to keep Health Connect data up to date between visits:
  - Android asks once for background access. If you decline, the switch stays off and NOOP keeps
    syncing when opened.
  - It runs on your chosen interval (6, 12 or 24 hours), only when the battery is not low, and reads
    just the last 3 days.
  - If background access is revoked later in Android settings, the switch turns itself off.

## Stress

- **The hour after activity is shaded** on the stress card and the stress widget, with a short note.
  Heart rate can stay up for a while after you stop moving, so readings in that hour may still be
  settling. This only changes how the chart looks: the stress values, and what AI Widget Maker
  receives, are unchanged.

## Foldables and tablets

- **Book posture.** A foldable half-open with the hinge running top to bottom keeps screens to one side
  of the hinge, with the navigation rail on the far edge of the other side. No text sits in the
  crease.
- The guide pages (How NOOP works, How your scores work, 4.0 vs 5.0/MG) and Steps calibration use a
  readable centred column on tablets instead of stretching edge to edge.

## Look and feel

- **Full Material You (optional).** With **Use wallpaper colours** on, a new **Backgrounds too**
  switch also tints the page background, cards and dividers from your wallpaper (Android 12 and
  later). It is off by default. Charts, scores and other data colours never change.
- **Your wallpaper on every full page.** The guides, Steps calibration, Live session and Live workout
  now show your sky or background image, under a soft wash that keeps the text easy to read.

## Release safety

- A release can no longer go out under an existing version number when the app has changed, and the
  test suite now runs before every release build.
- A weekly check reports new fixes in the original NOOP (ryanbr/noop) that could be merged in.

Built and checked with the Android unit test suite. Background sync and book posture are new, and they
have not yet been tested on every device. Please report what you find.
