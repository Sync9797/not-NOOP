# Not-Noop v12.3.2

Fixes for the most-reported Android problems in the original NOOP, a Workouts tab, and an easier way to
report bugs.

## New

- **Workouts tab.** Workouts has its own button on the bottom bar, the large-screen sidebar and the
  half-folded button pad, between Sleep and Coach. It was two taps deep in More before.
- **Report a bug from Settings.** Settings › Support explains what happens, then saves your strap log to
  your Downloads folder and opens a new bug report on this project's GitHub. Your app version, phone,
  Android version and (for a WHOOP 4.0) strap model are already filled in. Describe what happened, attach
  the saved log and press Submit. The log has serial numbers and Bluetooth addresses removed, you need a
  free GitHub account, and nothing is sent until you submit it yourself. On Android 8 and 9 the log
  opens in the share sheet instead, so you can save it first.

## Fixes

- **History sync no longer freezes until you force-stop NOOP** (reported upstream as ryanbr/noop #2805
  and #2908). Android sends Bluetooth commands to the strap one at a time, and some phones occasionally
  lose the "done" signal for one. Everything after it then waited forever, so the chunk counter froze
  and later syncs found nothing, while live heart rate carried on. NOOP now moves on after 15 seconds,
  and if it happens a second time on the same connection it reconnects, which is what force-stopping
  did. Each occurrence writes a "Write watchdog" line to the strap log. This needs confirming on more
  phones; please report if a sync still stalls.
- **Home-screen widgets stay filled while the strap is away** (#2807). The widgets only updated while
  the strap was connected or NOOP was open, so they could go blank when the strap was out of range or
  charging, and stay blank. They now refresh every 15 minutes while the strap is disconnected, keep the
  last battery and heart rate (dimmed) and the "last seen" time, and roll over to the new day's scores.
- **Mood logs to the right day.** If the screen was still open from the evening, a mood logged after
  midnight replaced the previous day's mood, and that day's mood showed as today's. NOOP now checks the
  date when you return to the screen and when you tap.
- **The morning recap arrives** (#2289). A recap held back until 05:00 could be lost if nothing
  re-checked it after 05:00. It is now scheduled for 05:00, and still never posted twice.

Built and checked with the Android unit test suite. The Bluetooth and widget fixes have not yet been
tested on every device; please use Report a bug if anything still goes wrong.
